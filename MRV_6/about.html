Mike Ray is an actor, filmmaker, writer, and producer based in Atlanta, with a body of work that spans performance, voice-over, and the development of original film and television projects. A seasoned creative with a grounded approach to the work, he has continued to build his career through experience, perseverance, and a clear commitment to meaningful storytelling.

"Every story deserves to be told the right way — and I won't stop until it is."

Born in Fairbanks, Alaska and raised in a military family, Mike lived throughout the United States and Germany before making Atlanta his professional base. His father, an Army sergeant who passed away from ALS at 46, remains a profound influence on his life and work, and serves as the emotional backdrop for Letters to My Father, a screenplay written from a deeply personal place.

Mike's original projects also include Heirs and In The Zone — a sitcom set inside a barbershop — both of which reflect his interest in character-driven stories rooted in community and culture. His festival-touring work, including the documentary The Helen Lindsey Story and the episodic feature The Productive Lie, demonstrates his ability to produce compelling, cinematic work on independent budgets.

On screen, Mike has appeared in House of Payne, Zatima, First Wives Club, Dynasty, The Falcon and the Winter Soldier, Saints & Sinners, Greenleaf, and more. As a voice artist with over 15 years of experience, he has narrated Carl Weber's bestselling audiobook The Man in 3B and continues to work across commercial, corporate, and character voiceover.

Away from the camera, Mike is a comedian, a former radio host, and the creator of the board game Journey to the Motherland. He brings the same energy to every room — whether on set, in the booth, or on stage.
